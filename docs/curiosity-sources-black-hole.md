## Original derivations and diagrams

- Schwarzschild radius, area entropy and the first-law check retain SI constants. The solar-mass radius, temperature and dimensionless entropy are rounded illustrative substitutions, not new observational measurements.
- The Bell-pair partial trace is an original worked calculation: each qubit has entropy `ln 2`, while the pair has zero entropy.
- The Page discussion distinguishes the exact finite-dimensional Schmidt bound from the typical-state approximation and coarse-grained entropy.
- The mass-loss example assumes constant `alpha`, a Schwarzschild hole and the additional idealized equality between coarse radiation entropy and lost black-hole entropy. Its `1 - 2^(-3/2) ≈ 0.646` fraction is explicitly not a realistic universal Page time.
- The island formula uses `c = k_B = 1`, extremization before minimization, the empty-island candidate and consistent renormalization.
- `public/images/diagrams/bh-info-correlations.svg` and `bh-info-page-balance.svg` are original repository SVG code. They use symbolic labels, no scripts or external resources, accessible title/description, and localized article alt text plus visible captions.
