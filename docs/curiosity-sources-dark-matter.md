## Verified primary sources

| [Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert, SPARC (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09251) | Rotation data and photometric/gas mass modelling; mass-to-light and disc-geometry dependence. Not evidence that every galaxy is exactly spherical or has an exactly flat curve. |
| [Clowe et al., Bullet Cluster (2006)](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0608407) | Lensing peaks displaced from dominant X-ray baryons. Supports additional relatively collisionless mass; article carefully avoids claiming identified WIMPs or excluding every modified-gravity theory. |
| [Planck Collaboration, 2018 results VI, corrected v4 (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209) | Spatially flat base LambdaCDM: Omega_c h²=0.120±0.001, Omega_b h²=0.0224±0.0001, h≈0.674. Errors 68%. Ratios computed in article, not independent measurements. Neutrino contribution distinguished. |
| [Steigman, Dasgupta & Beacom (2012)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.3622) | Approximate thermal abundance normalization and mass dependence. Radiation domination, entropy conservation, self-conjugate species and approximately velocity-independent annihilation specified. No universal WIMP mass range asserted. |
| [Peccei & Quinn (1977)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.38.1440) | Strong-CP motivation of axion mechanism, distinct from generic axion-like particles. DOI resolves to APS original paper. |
| [ADMX, Du et al. (2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.05750) | Cavity conversion search method. Used as a concrete method example, not claimed as latest exclusion. The 4 μeV to 0.97 GHz conversion is an original worked calculation. |
| [Dodelson & Widrow (1993/1994)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9303287) | Sterile-neutrino production proposal; treated as a candidate model. Warm behaviour depends on momentum distribution, not mass alone. |
| [Spergel & Steinhardt (1999/2000)](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9909386) | Self-interacting dark matter motivation, comparing halo/cluster scales. Historical proposal title is not adopted as a declaration of current discovery. |
| [LZ, 4.2 tonne-years, published 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.17036) | Historical SI WIMP–nucleon exclusion 2.2×10⁻⁴⁸ cm² at 40 GeV/c², 90% confidence, model-dependent. Explicitly not a universal exclusion or the latest limit in every channel. Distinct from annihilation cross section times velocity. |
| [Fermi-LAT dwarf analysis (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02641) | Illustrates model/final-state-dependent indirect limits and density-profile assumptions, not a latest-result claim. |
| [McGaugh, Lelli & Schombert (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.05917) | Observed radial-acceleration relation as a target for both modified dynamics and dark-matter galaxy formation. MOND low-acceleration v⁴=GM_ba_0 derived explicitly as a model relation. |
| [LZ extended recoil search, preprint 2026-09-02](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02823) | One ≈248 keV recoil candidate; 2.6σ global after look-elsewhere, 3.4σ maximum local. | [LZ collaboration homepage](https://lz.lbl.gov/) | Primary cross-check of September 2026 status and preprint destination. No time-sensitive “all results null” assertion retained. |
| [LZ light-dark-matter/solar-neutrino result, v3 2026-07-17](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08065) | Solar-neutrino recoil background is experimentally relevant; separate low-energy analysis from the high-energy September excess. |
| [O'Hare, neutrino floor (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.03116) | Corroborates that neutrino background is a loss of discrimination/sensitivity scaling, not an absolute detection barrier. |
| [ATLAS monojet search (2021; v2 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.10874) | Missing-transverse-momentum methodology and model-dependent interpretations. No claim of current collider discovery. |
| [Cooke, Pettini & Steidel (2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.11129) | Primordial deuterium plus nuclear calculation supplies independent baryon-density information. Supports the baryon-replacement thought experiment. |

## Original calculations and diagrams

- Circular Newtonian dynamics gives M(<r)=rv²/G under the stated spherical assumption. A flat curve gives local rho∝r⁻², not an infinite halo.
- G=4.30×10⁻⁶ kpc (km/s)² / solar mass, r=20 kpc, v=200 km/s gives 1.86×10¹¹ solar masses. Subtracting 6×10¹⁰ gives 1.26×10¹¹. Synthetic example, not data.
- Planck parameter ratios give Omega_c≈0.264, Omega_b≈0.049, Omega_c/Omega_b≈5.36; cold matter fraction of c+b≈0.843.
- 4 μeV divided by Planck constant gives approximately 0.967 GHz. Distinct symbols h and h_P avoid ambiguity.
- Elastic recoil maximum 2μ²v²/m_N uses c=1. Rate is per detector mass for a single isotope, with normalized detector-frame velocities and response effects explicitly discussed.
- dark-matter-deep-rotation.svg: original normalized spherical toy-model curves, solid/dashed as well as color distinction; numerical/symbolic visible labels. Both panels use r/r0 from 1 to 4.
- dark-matter-deep-merger.svg: original conceptual pre/post cluster passage; gas shapes, galaxy points and mass contours are visually distinct. No observational pixels, reconstructed contours, external content, or scripts. Figure size is not a measured mass encoding.
- Both assets have title/desc/role, a fixed high-contrast light drawing surface, and fully localized alt text plus explanatory visible captions in all ten editions.
