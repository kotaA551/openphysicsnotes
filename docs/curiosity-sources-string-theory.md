| 8 | [Juan Maldacena, The Large N Limit of Superconformal Field Theories and Supergravity, 1997/1998](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9711200) | The original AdS/CFT proposal and its type-IIB/AdS5 × S5 example; N and coupling limits. The gauge theory is not equated to the Standard Model or our cosmological spacetime. |
| 9 | [Edward Witten, Anti De Sitter Space and Holography, 1998](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9802150) | Boundary-source dictionary and correlation functions. | 10 | [Frederik Denef and Michael Douglas, Distributions of Flux Vacua, 2004](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0404116) | A concrete vacuum-counting problem with model assumptions. The article rejects an exact or universal interpretation of the often quoted 10^500 figure. |

The article's date is the research cutoff, not a claim to have exhaustively reviewed every paper published by that date. It makes no numerical collider bound, universal string-scale prediction or asserted cosmological detection.

## Conventions and scientific corrections

- Natural units: hbar = c = 1. String length is sqrt(alpha prime); string tension and dimensionless string coupling are separate parameters.
- The displayed mode expansion uses sigma period 2 pi, alpha oscillators multiplying exp[-in(tau−sigma)] and tilde-alpha oscillators multiplying exp[-in(tau+sigma)].
- Circle winding is Y(sigma+2 pi) = Y(sigma) + 2 pi w R. With pL = n/R + wR/alpha prime and pR = n/R − wR/alpha prime, N counts alpha excitations and N-tilde counts tilde-alpha excitations. Consequently N − N-tilde − nw = 0, and the two separate mass conditions are displayed. An initial opposite-sign convention was corrected following independent review.
- The physical quantum Virasoro conditions are distinguished from the classical constraints. The second sector is called the tilde sector rather than assigned an inconsistent propagation direction.
- The spectrum example is the uncompactified closed bosonic string with intercept one. Superstring sectors and GSO projections are not assigned that same spectrum without qualification.
- The five familiar theories are identified as ten-dimensional supersymmetric perturbative theories. Heterotic internal degrees of freedom are not presented as sixteen extra macroscopic spatial dimensions. RR potential degrees and field-strength degrees are distinguished.
- Nc is defined as the number of colors in SU(Nc), not the exact Lie-group rank. Large lambda and small string coupling control separate corrections in the gravity approximation.
- Duality checks, protected entropy counting, phenomenological model tests and direct experimental confirmation are kept distinct. No fixed total number of vacua, observed supersymmetry, universal Planck-scale string tension or generic singularity resolution is asserted.
